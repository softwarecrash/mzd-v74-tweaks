The installer on the bench also contained the Config feature branch. Its bundle
helper was combined with this PR's executable support, preserving mutable-config
handling. Isolated tests of that combination passed 22 real package actions,
31 bundle/reset checks, bounded-log behavior, FAT-like metadata and critical
chmod-failure rollback. The exact local loader adaptation is reproducible through
`tools/update_tplink_rtl8811au.py --check`; the kernel module is unchanged.

## Limits

No AP/client connection, scan, throughput, wireless Android Auto, repeated cold-boot
or vehicle ACC/power-cycle test is claimed. Removal of 1.0.2 after a boot with the
module loaded has not yet been tested. Raw device logs and firmware stay private.

## Follow-up: bounded persistent diagnostics (1.0.3)

The later 1.0.3 loader restores persistent diagnostics under `/data_persist/log/`
for correlation with AAW after reboot. It keeps the current and previous boot
breadcrumbs (4 KiB each) and the latest failure dump (40 lines / 8 KiB, including
its boot ID). Retries in the same boot do not rotate away the previous boot.
Uninstall and Restore originals remove all three driver-owned files.

This follow-up has isolated loader and package coverage; the physical CMU boot
results above describe 1.0.2 and do not verify the new persistent logging policy.
